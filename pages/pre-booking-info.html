What is pre-booking?

Pre-booking lets you hold your place in the 1% Club by paying a small amount now. You do not have to pick your courses or pay the full fee today. You do both later, when you are ready.

The amount you pay today is not an extra charge. It counts towards the membership price, so you only pay the rest later.

How it works, step by step

  1. Pay the pre-booking amount

    You pay securely through Razorpay. Your seat is reserved as soon as the payment is confirmed.

  2. Choose your courses

    After your payment is confirmed, you pick your courses from the list of eligible courses. You can change your choice any time before you pay the remaining balance.

  3. Pay the remaining balance

    This is the membership price minus the amount you already paid. You can do this whenever you are ready.

  4. Your membership starts

    Once the full amount is paid, your membership is active and you get access to the courses you chose.

Good to know

  • Pre-booking reserves your seat only. You will not get course access until the full membership price is paid.
  • Your booking keeps the membership price and the number of courses that applied when you pre-booked.
  • You can have one open pre-booking at a time. If you already have one, Continue takes you back to it.
  • Coupons cannot be used on a pre-booking.
  • If you are already a 1% Club member, you do not need to pre-book.

Terms and conditions

  1. The pre-booking amount reserves one seat in the 1% Club for your account. It does not give you access to any course or membership benefit.
  2. Your membership becomes active only after the full membership price has been paid.
  3. To complete your booking, you must choose the exact number of courses your plan requires, from the courses offered at that time.
  4. All payments are processed securely by Razorpay. Your booking is confirmed only after the payment is successful.
  5. Refunds and cancellations are handled under our Refund Policy.
  6. The membership price shown is a limited-time offer. Prices for new buyers can change once the offer ends.
  7. By continuing, you also agree to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
