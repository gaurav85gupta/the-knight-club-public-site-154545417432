Where it began

Learning should lead somewhere.

The Knight Club started with its founder, Aditya Srivastava, and a simple observation from his own path: he began building skills straight after finishing 12th grade, and he learned them by using them — not by waiting for a certificate, a job or anyone’s permission.

That experience shaped a question the platform still asks: what if the practical, modern skills people need — marketing, content, freelancing, editing, AI tools, building for the web — were taught clearly, in order, and with real application at the centre?

The answer became a set of structured courses, taught by people who do the work, on a platform designed to stay out of the way of learning. We are still building, and we would rather describe that honestly than dress it up as a legend.

Philosophy

What We Believe

Four ideas sit behind every course we publish.

  1. 01

    Skills are learned by using them

    Watching is a start, not the finish. A skill becomes yours when you apply it to something real, so our lessons are built around doing.

  2. 02

    Learning shouldn’t wait for permission

    You don’t need a title or a certain stage of life to begin learning something useful. You need a clear path and a reason to start.

  3. 03

    Structure makes skills teachable

    Ideas stick when they arrive in order. Modules and lessons let each step build on the last, so you always know what comes next and why.

  4. 04

    Practical skills sit beside formal education

    Schools and colleges lay foundations. We add the hands-on layer next to them — one does not replace the other.

Our idea in one line

School for what school forgot.

What “school” means here

The word points to the place where people first learn how to learn. We respect that. The Knight Club is built to sit alongside it, not to argue with it.

What “forgot” means

It isn’t a complaint. Curricula are broad by design, and modern tools change faster than any syllabus can follow. Some practical skills simply end up outside the classroom.

How it shows up in our courses

Digital marketing, content creation, freelancing, AI tools, video editing, digital products and web design — skills you can use on real work. Explore our courses.

How we teach

How courses are built

From Philosophy to Finished Course

Every course follows the same path before a learner sees it: philosophy, course design, structured lessons, practical application, learner progress.

  • 01

    Founder-led, practitioner-taught

    Learn from people connected to the work. Courses are built by those who do the skill, not those who only teach it.

  • 02

    Transparent before you buy

    Curriculum, instructor, level and price are visible before you enrol. You decide with full information.

  • 03

    Try before you commit

    Where a course offers free-preview lessons, you can get a feel for it before spending anything.

  • 04

    Progress you can see

    Your progress is tracked as you learn. Pick up exactly where you stopped, every time.
