Why practical skills matter

The World Changed. Learning Had To Change Too.

Education gives you a foundation. Practical skills help you build on it.

Schools give you a foundation. But work moved fast — AI reshaped everyday workflows, businesses live online, and tools change every few months. That's learned by doing, not by sitting in a lecture.

That's the space The Knight Club works in: not a replacement for formal education, but the practical layer beside it. School for what school forgot.
